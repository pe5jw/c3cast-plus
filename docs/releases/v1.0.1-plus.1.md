# C3CAST-plus 1.0.1-plus.1

The first release of **C3CAST-plus**, PE5JW's extended version of [C3CAST](https://github.com/HB9IIU/C3CAST) 1.0.1 by HB9IIU,
with the [ESP32-DATV](https://github.com/SP8ESA/ESP32-DATV) firmware by SP8ESA. Everything C3CAST does still works the same way.
Why each change was made: see the [README](https://github.com/pe5jw/c3cast-plus#readme).

## What's new

- **Band profiles** - QO-100 (as before) or **2.4 GHz direct**: receive your own signal on 13 cm, without the satellite.
- **Adjustable LO and correction** per profile and per kind of receiver, for other LNBs and for downconverters such as the
  Nooelec Ham It Down.
- **MiniTiouner direct on 2.4 GHz** (LO 0), with the LNB supply always off.
- **RTL-SDR bias-T switch**, remembered per profile, to power a converter through the coax.
- **HackRF One receiver**, with LNA / VGA gain, RF amp and antenna power settings.
- **Receiver assignment**: each receiver Automatic, Not used or pinned to one tuner; **Rescan USB** without restarting.
- **Optional Receiver 3**, and a receive row per receiver in the 2.4 GHz profile.

## Install

Download `C3CAST-plus-Portable-x64-1.0.1-plus.1.zip`, unzip it anywhere and double-click **C3CAST-plus.exe**. Windows 10/11, no
installation. The `.sha256` file holds the zip's checksum. Your settings live in `config.json` next to the exe: to keep the
settings of an earlier C3CAST, copy its `config.json` there.

**Quit with the END button** on the page, so the receivers release their tuners.

Licensed radio amateurs only. Use suitable RF filtering before any amplifier, and protect your receivers when you receive your
own transmitter at short range.
